Plan and present classroom topics as fictional series. SeriesCreator is free to use.

[Buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/haake)

This link opens Buy Me a Coffee in a new tab. The page sends no data to the
provider before you follow the link. After it opens, the provider's
[privacy policy](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/privacy-policy) applies.

Contributions help keep the website running, maintain the app, fix bugs, and
test across browsers and for accessibility. Using SeriesCreator requires no
account. Contributions are voluntary and do not unlock additional features.

## Help without donating

- [Report a bug](https://haak3.atlassian.net/jira/software/form/69a50efc-0474-445e-8f01-66eb6b747cc1)
- [Suggest a feature](https://haak3.atlassian.net/jira/software/form/50d4fc51-bff4-4e21-b46e-8fd987b90d24)

The forms open in a new tab. Leave out project content and personal data.

For other questions, email [apps@haak3.de](mailto:apps@haak3.de?subject=SeriesCreator%20Feedback).

You can also [report bugs on GitHub](https://github.com/ChristianHaake/SeriesCreator/issues)
or share the project with other educators. Include your browser and the steps
to reproduce the problem. Do not publish personal data from classroom projects
in a report.
