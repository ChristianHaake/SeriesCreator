SeriesCreator is free to use and requires no account. Financial contributions
are voluntary and do not unlock additional features.

## What contributions support

Contributions support running the website, maintenance, bug fixes, and testing
across browsers and for accessible use.

[Support SeriesCreator through Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/haake)

This link opens an external provider. No data is sent to Buy Me a Coffee before
you follow the link. After opening it, the provider's own
[privacy policy](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/privacy-policy) applies.

## Help without donating

You can [report bugs on GitHub](https://github.com/ChristianHaake/SeriesCreator/issues),
suggest improvements, or share the project with other educators. Include your
browser and the steps to reproduce a problem. Do not publish personal data from
classroom projects in a report.
